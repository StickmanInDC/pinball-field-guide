The Hobbit

Pick a rulesheet section. Each one is a short run of steps, and the playfield highlights the shots for the step you are on. Use the arrow keys to move between steps.

Jersey Jack Pinball's support site lists The Hobbit as a Limited Edition, a Special Edition and a Standard Edition, and links the same game manual and the same code update for all three. No source I found lists a different playfield layout or different rules, so this guide shows one playfield for every edition. The differences between editions appear to be cosmetic, but I could not find a JJP comparison sheet that says so.

The rulesheet was written early in the game's life, gives no code revision, and leaves most of the 31 modes undescribed. Shot positions come from the shot maps and switch location drawings in JJP's game manual (November 2018), and shot names follow the rulesheet. Where the rulesheet and the manual disagree the guide says so. Where the rulesheet is silent the guide says "not documented yet".

How far to trust the highlights
  • The picture is Jersey Jack Pinball's own straight-down photo of the Limited Edition playfield, from its distributor photo folder, cropped below the flippers. No perspective correction was needed. Smaug, the ramps, the book and the wireforms stand up off the playfield, so parts under them are hidden.
  • I lined the manual's layout drawings up with the photo using the LOCK rollovers, the inserts above the flippers and the captive balls (errors of a few pixels). Most estimated positions are the drawing's positions carried onto the photo, so raised parts may be a little off.
  • Each highlighted feature carries a tag. Clear means a printed label, an insert or an obvious part identifies it in the photo. Estimated means I placed it from the manual's drawings or from context and it is worth confirming on the machine.
  • Clear: Smaug, the book display, both ramp entrances and their BOOK, TIME, LOCK and MODE cutouts, the captive balls, the DWARF and MAN drop targets, the four beast panels, the LOCK rollovers, the upper right flipper, the inlanes and outlanes, the inserts above the flippers and the shooter lane.
  • Estimated: both orbit paths and spinners, the ramp paths past their entrances, the left wire ramp, the ELF drop targets (under a wireform), the nine dwarf targets behind the drops, both VUK holes, the Thorin target, the Mystery target, the Bag End lane and the three pop bumpers.

Sources: Rulesheet, JJP's game manual (section A.2, rules and shot maps, and the playfield switch locations in section C), JJP's rules flow chart, and the Limited Edition playfield photo from Jersey Jack Pinball.
