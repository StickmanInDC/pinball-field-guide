Total Nuclear Annihilation

Pick a rulesheet section. Each one is a short run of steps, and the playfield highlights the shots for the step you are on. Use the arrow keys to move between steps.

The rulesheet dates Total Nuclear Annihilation to January 2017. A later "Welcome to the Future" run is also reported, but I could not confirm from Spooky how it differs. The two playfield images I could check (the 2017 photo and a bare playfield Spooky listed in 2023) show the same layout, and the rulesheet does not split its rules by version, so this guide shows one playfield.

The rulesheet gives no code revision. A few of its entries are marked as unknown; this guide says "not documented yet" where that is the case.

How far to trust the highlights
  • The picture is Spooky's own photo of a finished machine, from its original game page. It was taken at an angle from the front, so I corrected the perspective to make the playfield look straight down. Posts, the pop bumper and other parts that stand up off the playfield lean a little in the result, and the top of the playfield is upscaled.
  • Each highlighted feature carries a tag. Clear means a printed label, insert or toy identifies it in the picture. Estimated means I placed it from context and it is worth confirming on the machine.
  • The rulesheet has a layout section, and shots were placed from it and the photo. The apron instruction card in the photo also helped: it calls the main scoop the left scoop and says balls lock in the drop targets.
  • The CORE rollovers, the reactor area, the mini flipper, the scoop, the grid targets and grid, the 1 to 9 inserts, the spinner, the RAD and Destroy targets, the pop bumper, the lanes, the bonus inserts and the flippers are clear. The orbit paths, the orbit gates, the reactor's 1, 2, 3 targets, kickers and display, the lock lane, its drop targets, scoop and arrows, the slingshots, the ball save display and the shooter lane are estimated.

Sources: Rulesheet and playfield photo (Spooky Pinball's site, archived by the Wayback Machine).
