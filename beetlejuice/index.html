Beetlejuice

Pick a rulesheet section. Each one is a short run of steps, and the playfield highlights the shots for the step you are on. Use the arrow keys to move between steps.

Spooky Pinball sells Beetlejuice as a Collector's Edition. Its store lists no other model, so this guide shows one playfield.

The rulesheet gives no code revision and is still being written. Singing in the Stairwell's qualifying rule comes from Spooky's v2026.04.10 release notes. The Inferno Room wizard mode, the Scare-o-meter, the Sandworm battles Gut Punch and Sharp Shooter, and how a Juno mode starts are not documented yet.

How far to trust the highlights
  • The picture is not a photo of a finished machine. It is the straight-down playfield render from Spooky's "Frightful Features" sales flyer, which notes that a prototype playfield is shown and that some things may have changed for production. It is fairly small (630 pixels wide), and some parts, including the pop bumpers and the spinner, are hidden under ramps or not drawn. Spooky's only real playfield photo is taken at a steep angle from the front, so I used it only to identify parts.
  • Each highlighted feature carries a tag. Clear means a printed label, insert or toy identifies it in the picture. Estimated means I placed it from context and it is worth confirming on the machine.
  • The inserts (Scare, Lost Soul, Knock, Juno, Now Serving, camera, 1 to 5, Mode Start, Mystery, mode and Sandworm inserts), the left ramp, the wheel, the couch, the camera, the Sandworm and its target bank, the tombstone, the coffin and the covered bridge are clear. Spooky's manual (lamp and switch lists) and flyer were used to name several of them.
  • The orbits, the right ramp, the spinner, the pops, the Upper Loop, the Juno Scoop, the Now Serving targets, the upper flipper, the camera targets, the center scoop doors, the drop target, the captive ball, the Mystery Scoop, the Mode Change switch, the Handbook and Extra Ball targets, the lanes, slingshots, flippers and magnet are estimated.

Sources: Rulesheet, Spooky Pinball (release notes), Maintenance manual, Switch chart, flyer as reposted by Retro Refurbs.
