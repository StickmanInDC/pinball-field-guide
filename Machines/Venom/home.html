The rulesheet treats Premium and LE as one game and notes where the Pro differs. Each model has its own photo.

How far to trust the highlights
  • Rules come from the supplied rulesheet, based on code 1.06. It has no layout text, so the shot order comes from its lists and the printed lane names.
  • Each highlighted feature carries a tag. Clear means a printed label, insert or toy identifies it in the photo. Estimated means I placed it from context and it is worth confirming on the machine.
  • Stern's apron card is readable on the Pro photo. It ties the center ramp to the Police Station lane and the scoop to the Daily Bugle lane. The Choose Host insert ties the left ramp to the Alchemax lane. The other lanes are read in order, and most paths past the arrows are guesses.
  • The left orbit, left loop, right loop, right orbit, bell tower target, canisters, bottom lanes and Premium and LE U-turn ramp and flyaway targets are worth checking.
  • Only two of the five red targets are placed. The top lanes, rollover lanes, Secret Hideout saucer, sinkhole, pop bumpers, spinners and slingshots are not marked, because I could not find them in the photos.
  • The rulesheet does not say which two shots are the green Mayhem locks.
