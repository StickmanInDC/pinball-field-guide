Pick your machine above, then a rulesheet section. The artwork changes to match. The playfield highlights the shots for the step you are on, and the arrow keys move between steps.

The rulesheet treats Premium and LE as one game. It gives the Premium and LE a magnetic Telekinesis lock that holds up to two balls for a bigger super jackpot, and a swipe-a-ball with the action button; the Pro locks a ball behind an up-post. Each model has its own photo.

The rulesheet is for code 1.09. It does not describe the Final Showdown wizard mode, and in a few places it uses two names for one shot or gives two different numbers. The guide marks those gaps instead of filling them in.

How far to trust the highlights
  • Rules come from the supplied rulesheet, based on code 1.09. Its Layout section is only photos, so the shot names on the playfield are my reading of the inserts and of Stern's feature list.
  • Each highlighted feature carries a tag. Clear means a printed label, insert or toy identifies it in the photo. Estimated means I placed it from context and it is worth confirming on the machine.
  • The shot entrances are fixed by their inserts: Lock Ball under the left ramp, Extra Ball, Demo Dog Attack and Mystery under the left standup lane, the five Demogorgon modes up the middle, Burn It Back on the right inner loop, and 2X Scoring at the saucer. The Demogorgon, the center ramp, the DEMODOG letters and the six objectives are clear.
  • The left orbit and right ramp are my reading of the bicycle and binocular chapter arrows. The paths of the orbits, ramps and loop past their entrances, the left standup target, the center scoop and the pop bumpers are estimated. The spinner, slingshots, the standups beside the right ramp and the Premium and LE magnet are not marked.
  • In Stern's Pro photo the Hawkins Laboratory ramp sits over the drop targets and the M and O letters, so those are hidden. The rulesheet says the Pro holds a locked ball behind an up-post on the left ramp.
  • In the Premium and LE photos the center ramp shows a projected image, and the WILL drop targets and the M and O letters are visible in front of it. Stern lists the projector, a shaking Demogorgon and the Eleven Telekinesis magnetic ball lock for these models.

Sources: Rulesheet, Stern Pro, Stern Premium.
