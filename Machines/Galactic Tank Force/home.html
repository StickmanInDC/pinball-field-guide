Pick a rulesheet section. Each one is a short run of steps, and the playfield highlights the shots for the step you are on. Use the arrow keys to move between steps.

American Pinball sold Deluxe, Limited (Signature) and Victory editions. Its page says the Victory keeps the Deluxe's core playfield; the editions differ in cabinet, armor, topper and toys, and the Victory has a new tank sculpt. This guide shows one playfield for all of them.

The rulesheet gives no code revision and is unfinished. Its Overview and Layout sections are blank, Extra Balls and End-of-Ball Bonus say only "TBD", and the three wizard modes have qualifying rules but no play rules. American Pinball's page says later code adds extra balls and the Tank Zulu mini-wizard mode, which this guide does not cover.

How far to trust the highlights
  • The photo is an owner's overhead shot, which I take to be from the machine's Pinside gallery, taken at an angle from the lower end. It is sharp, but it is cut off just above the LAB sign, so the top of the playfield (the Space Station AIR Lock and the Planet Spinner) is not shown. It has not been straightened, so the upper playfield looks a little compressed.
  • Each highlighted feature carries a tag. Clear means a printed label, insert or toy identifies it in the photo. Estimated means I placed it from context and it is worth confirming on the machine.
  • The insert columns, the wizard inserts, the flank targets and their award inserts, the AIR targets, the enemy tank and its target bank, the UFO, the bumpers, the LAB sign, the flippers, the atomic shield and the Lucky Break lanes are clear. I used American Pinball's labeled sell sheet to name several of them.
  • Both orbits, the left ramp, the right saucer, the lab saucer, the LAB lanes, the accelerator targets, the AIR spinner, the return lanes and the plunger lane are estimated. The Planet Spinner and the advance rank lanes are not marked.

Sources: Rulesheet, American Pinball.
