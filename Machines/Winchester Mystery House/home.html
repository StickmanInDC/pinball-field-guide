Pick a rulesheet section. Each one is a short run of steps, and the playfield highlights the shots for the step you are on. Use the arrow keys to move between steps.

The rulesheet says 525 machines were made, a run Barrels of Fun's flyer also gives ("limited up to 525 games"). It describes one model, so this guide shows one playfield.

The rulesheet is based on code revision 2026.10.01. It has no layout section and links no pictures, and I could not find a Barrels of Fun game manual or rules map for this machine. So most shots here are my placement from the photo, guided by the playfield's printed inserts. Where the rulesheet is silent the guide says "not documented yet".

How far to trust the highlights
  • The picture is Barrels of Fun's own photo of the playfield, from the back of its Winchester Mystery House flyer. It was taken from the front at a slight angle, so I corrected the perspective. The flyer lays round close-up pictures over the playfield: one covers the top right corner and the right orbit, one the right side around the upper flipper, and one the left side above the crystal ball. Those areas are hidden. Ramps, wireforms and the house sculpts stand up off the playfield, so they lean in the result.
  • Each highlighted feature carries a tag. Clear means a printed insert, label or obvious part identifies it in the photo. Estimated means I placed it from context and it is worth confirming on the machine. Most shots are estimated.
  • Clear: the flippers, the O-P-E-N lane inserts, the Return insert, the KEY inserts, the three center drop target inserts, the Annunciator insert, the 1-2-3 Seance Room inserts, the Help insert, the crystal ball and the room inserts on the map.
  • Estimated: every ramp, loop, orbit and hallway shot, the spinner, the turntable, the Stairs to Nowhere, the tower, the drop targets themselves, the instrument targets, the mini flipper, the slingshots and the lanes. Not placed at all: the captive ball, the X target, the hidden bass and treble targets, the Door to Nowhere ramp and the center (side) ramp. I could not find them in the photo.

Sources: Rulesheet (edited by Fudd) and Barrels of Fun's Winchester Mystery House flyer (feature list and playfield photo).
