The rulesheet treats the three models as one game, with two notes: the Pro's right ramp can collect several ghosts at once, and on the Premium and LE Slimer can end up in the Ecto Goggles. Stern's features list shows bigger hardware differences, so each model has its own photo.

How far to trust the highlights
  • Rules come from the community rulesheet, which mentions code 1.16. It has no Layout section.
  • Shot positions come from Stern's service manuals. Their lamp lists name an arrow for each shot (LT Scoop, Left Orbit, Left Ramp, Right Orbit, Right Ramp, Subway) and their diagrams show where the switches sit. I matched the diagrams to the photo by eye, so treat every lane as approximate.
  • Each highlighted feature carries a tag. Clear means a printed label, insert or toy identifies it in the photo. Estimated means I placed it from the manual or from context and it is worth confirming on the machine.
  • The rulesheet and the manual use different names. I read the rulesheet's left and right loops as the manual's Left and Right Orbit, the right scoop as the Subway, the center target as the Ghost Target, the left captive balls as the "Public Library" Newton balls, and the right captive ball as the "Stacked Books" Newton ball (both names from Stern's features list). These are my readings.
  • The pop bumpers, the P top lane, the Gozer target, the center target and the targets in front of the left ramp are hidden or hard to see from above; they are placed from the manual.
  • The Scoleri Brothers drop targets are down in the photos, so their marks sit on the slots.
  • On the Premium and LE, the Ecto Goggles sit at the upper right and Slimer hangs a little lower. The 3D library model hides the left standups. The LE photo shows the same layout as the Premium with different trim.

Sources: Rulesheet, Stern Pro manual, Stern Premium/LE manual, Stern features list. The photos are Stern's own playfield shots from its older game pages.
