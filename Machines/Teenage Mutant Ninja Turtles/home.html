How far to trust the highlights
  • Rules come from the supplied rulesheet, based on code 1.50, though it also mentions 1.54 and 1.55. Its Layout section is only pictures, and Cowabunga Multiball is not described.
  • Each highlighted feature carries a tag. Clear means a printed label, insert or toy identifies it in the photo. Estimated means I placed it from context and it is worth confirming on the machine.
  • The Turtle arrows and the rulesheet's turtle table tie the left ramp, Pizza Parlor, center ramp and LAIR Lane to printed inserts. Most paths past the arrows are guesses.
  • Stern's Ninja Instructions card is readable on the Premium photo. Lines taken from it say so.
  • "Major shots" are listed by the rulesheet's Pizza-o-Meter Episode, and those six are marked wherever it says major shots.
  • The center ramp and Krang Loop could be swapped. The LAIR Lane, LAIR Target, APRIL Target, 2X Target, pop bumpers and top lanes are also worth checking. The Pizza Targets, spinner and slingshots are not marked.

Sources: Rulesheet, Stern.
