Pick a rulesheet section. Each one is a short run of steps, and the playfield highlights the shots for the step you are on. Use the arrow keys to move between steps.

Spooky Pinball sells Evil Dead as a Collector's Edition. Its store lists no other model, so this guide shows one playfield.

The rulesheet gives no code revision, has no layout section, and several of its entries are rough notes. Spooky has added a lot in later code (new wizard modes, Cheryl Multiball, a video mode and more), mostly not covered by the rulesheet. Where this guide names those, it quotes Spooky's release notes, and how they play is not documented yet.

How far to trust the highlights
  • The picture is not a photo of a finished machine. It is Spooky's own playfield render, the one on its "Deadly Details" sales flyer, which notes that a prototype playfield is shown and that some things may have changed for production. The render is drawn at an angle from the front, so I corrected the perspective to make the playfield look straight down. Toys and ramps that stand up off the playfield are a little stretched by that.
  • Each highlighted feature carries a tag. Clear means a printed label, insert or toy identifies it in the picture. Estimated means I placed it from context and it is worth confirming on the machine.
  • The rulesheet does not describe the layout. Shots were placed from the render, Spooky's flyers (which name the toys) and Spooky's switch chart (which names the switches). Spooky's release notes link the car ramp to the left ramp ("Delta 88") and the Dagger inserts to the orbits.
  • The Evil Hand, Linda's head, the cabin, the cellar door, the deadite hatches, the shotgun shells, the GROOVY targets, the fruit cellar lower playfield, the flippers and the inserts are clear. The orbits, ramp entrances, the scoops, the cellar targets, the cabin drop target, the necklace targets, the captive ball, the pops, the hidden sling flipper and the lanes are estimated.

Sources: Rulesheet, Spooky Pinball (release notes), Switch and coil chart, Game manual, Playfield render and feature flyer (Spooky's site, archived by the Wayback Machine).
