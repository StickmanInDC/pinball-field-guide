Pick your machine above, then a rulesheet section. The artwork changes to match. The playfield highlights the shots for the step you are on, and the arrow keys move between steps.

The rulesheet barely separates the models. Its only edition note is that a Sentinel Attack can also start at any shot that feeds the Sentinel hands on the Premium and LE. Stern lists a motorized Sentinel head and the Sentinel hand for those models. Each model has its own photo.

The rulesheet is for code 0.98 and is unfinished. The Master Mold wizard mode "will appear in a later code update", the Layout section is blank, and in a few places it uses two names for one shot or gives two different rules. The guide marks those gaps instead of filling them in.

How far to trust the highlights
  • Rules come from the supplied rulesheet, based on code 0.98. Its Layout section is blank, so the shot names on the playfield are my reading of the printed labels and of Stern's feature list.
  • Each highlighted feature carries a tag. Clear means a printed label, insert or toy identifies it in the photo. Estimated means I placed it from context and it is worth confirming on the machine.
  • The six city shots are fixed by their printed labels and arrows: X-Jet, Downtown, Uptown, Midtown, Subway and Highway, with the Beast Lab label between Uptown and Midtown. The Sentinel head, the eight mission inserts, the mini-flipper and the Cerebro dome are clear.
  • The ramp and loop paths past their entrances, the Danger Room spinner, exit ramp, Abort and bonus hold targets, both pop bumpers, the X-Mansion target, Kitty Pryde's lane, the return lanes and the Sentinel's mouth are estimated. The Nightcrawler (purple) targets, the Cerebro Bonus Build targets, the Sentinel targets, the slingshots and the left lanes are not marked.
  • On the Pro, the Beast's Lab captive ball is not clear in Stern's photo, so it is placed at the end of its lane.
  • On the Premium and LE, Stern's photos show the Sentinel hand on the right, a Wolverine figure at the top left, and a chrome captive ball under three green level lights at the end of the Beast Lab lane. Stern lists the Wolverine figure under LE features, but the Premium photo shows it too.

Sources: Rulesheet, Stern Pro, Stern Premium.
