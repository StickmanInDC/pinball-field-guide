How far to trust the highlights
  • Rules come from the supplied rulesheet. It names no code revision, and its only reference is a change made "as of 0.87", so the rules are 0.87 or later.
  • Each highlighted feature carries a tag. Clear means a label or toy identifies it in the photo. Estimated means I placed it from context and it is worth confirming on the machine.
  • Each main shot is placed from its character insert and arrow, using the rulesheet's list of which character goes with which shot. Those inserts are clear in the photo. The lanes beyond them are not, because the clear ramps hide much of the routing, so shot paths are Estimated.
  • The pop bumpers, the Groot standups, the Orb drop target and the standup behind it are my best reading of the photo. I could not identify any of them for certain.
  • Groot's hands on this photo cover several inserts on the left and right. Those spots line up with the same inserts on the Pro photo.
  • The rulesheet calls the Rocket shot a kickback and also says "moving-mouth Groot". It does not say whether either differs between models.
