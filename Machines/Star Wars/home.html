This is the 2017 Stern game, designed by Steve Ritchie. It is not Star Wars: Fall of the Empire, which has its own guide. Pick your machine above, then a rulesheet section. The artwork changes to match. The playfield highlights the shots for the step you are on, and the arrow keys move between steps.

The rulesheet treats Premium and LE as one game and notes where the Pro differs. Each model has its own photo.

How far to trust the highlights
  • Rules come from the supplied rulesheet, based on code 1.31. Its Layout section is blank, and its Pict-o-Pops list is unfinished.
  • Each highlighted feature carries a tag. Clear means a printed label, insert or toy identifies it in the photo. Estimated means I placed it from context and it is worth confirming on the machine.
  • The Death Star, Endor, Hoth and Tatooine cards fix four lanes, because the rulesheet ties each planet to a shot. The left orbit, right ramp and right orbit are my reading of the leftover arrows, and most paths past the arrows are guesses.
  • Stern's game instructions card is readable on the Premium photo. Lines taken from it say so.
  • "Major shots" are never listed. I mark the orbits, mini-loops and ramps, because the rulesheet counts 6 major shots.
  • The right scoop, the Jedi Training standups, the TIE Fighter target, the pop bumpers and the Hyperspace wireform are the least certain marks. Spinners, slingshots and the outlane gate are not marked.

Sources: Rulesheet, Stern.
