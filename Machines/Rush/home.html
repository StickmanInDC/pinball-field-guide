Pick your machine above, then a rulesheet section. The artwork changes to match. The playfield highlights the shots for the step you are on, and the arrow keys move between steps.

The rulesheet treats Premium and LE as one game, so they share rules, but each has its own photo. Steps that differ from the Pro show only on the model they apply to.

How far to trust the highlights
  • Rules come from the supplied rulesheet, based on code 1.14. It has no layout text, only photos.
  • Each highlighted feature carries a tag. Clear means a printed label or toy identifies it in the photo. Estimated means I placed it from context and it is worth confirming on the machine.
  • The shot lanes are matched by their 1-2-3 inserts. The numbers printed on each lane fit the rulesheet's combo table, which gives good support for the lanes, but clear ramps and wires hide most of the paths past the inserts.
  • The side loop and side ramp both carry a 2 and a 3, so those inserts cannot tell them apart. I placed them by where their arrows point, and they could be swapped.
  • I read both scoops as the two openings of the dice housing. The rulesheet says upper, top and side scoop for one, and left and bottom scoop for the other.
  • The pop bumpers, instrument standup, Lite and Lock standups, spinner, dead end and under-flipper shot are also my reading. I could not see the Premium and LE captive ball, the RUSH standups, or the ramp diverter.
  • The apron card helped name shots. It calls the right ramp the Hemispheres ramp and the target behind the drops the Freewill target.

Sources: Rulesheet, Stern.
