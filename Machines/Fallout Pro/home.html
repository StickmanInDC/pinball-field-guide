Pick a rulesheet section. Each one is a short run of steps, and the playfield on the right highlights the shots for the step you are on. Use the arrow keys to move between steps. This guide covers the Pro only.

How far to trust the highlights
  • Rules come from the supplied Pinball Rule Sheets page (code 0.81). The game is new and the rulesheet has gaps, including all three wizard modes. Those steps say so instead of guessing.
  • Each highlighted feature carries a tag. Clear means a label or toy identifies it in the photo. Estimated means I placed it from context and it is worth confirming on the machine.
  • Most shot paths are Estimated. I found no source that maps the rulesheet's shot names onto the playfield, and wire ramps hide much of the upper right. Trust the labeled inserts more than the ramp and loop paths.
  • The rulesheet uses both "left orbit" and "left ramp". I treat them as the same shot, which is my reading and not something it states.
  • The spinner, radio tower target, junk target and scoop are placed from the inserts that point at them. Slingshots are not highlighted.

Sources: Pinball Rule Sheets, Wizard Mod review, Arcade Heroes preview.
