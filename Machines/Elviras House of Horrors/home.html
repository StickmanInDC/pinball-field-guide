Pick a rulesheet section. Each one is a short run of steps, and the playfield highlights the shots for the step you are on. Use the arrow keys to move between steps.

Stern sold this game as a Premium, a Limited Edition and a Signature Edition. Stern's feature matrix gives all three the same playfield features, and its manual lists one bare playfield for all models; they differ in cabinet art, trim, backglass and playfield plastics and decals. This guide shows one playfield, from Stern's Premium photo.

How far to trust the highlights
  • Rules come from the community rulesheet, based on code 1.01. Its Layout section is only one photo, and several parts of it say "need info"; the guide says "not documented yet" where it does.
  • Shot positions come from Stern's LE/Premium manual. Its lamp list names an arrow for each of the seven shots (Left Loop, Left Ramp, Drop Target, House, Right Ramp, Crypt, Right Loop), and its switch and lamp diagrams show where the targets and lanes sit. I matched the diagrams to the photo with a rough fit (about 15 px error in the photo's full size), so treat every lane as approximate.
  • Each highlighted feature carries a tag. Clear means a printed label, insert or toy identifies it in the photo. Estimated means I placed it from the manual or from context and it is worth confirming on the machine.
  • Name readings that are mine, not stated by the rulesheet: the manual's Left Loop and Right Loop are the rulesheet's left and right orbits; the "Drop Target" arrow is the Garage shot, behind the gravestone drop target; the manual's Right Spinner (Stern calls it the Beast Booster) sits in the right orbit.
  • The four Gar-Goil targets are my reading: Stern's parts list has four Gargoyle Leaper Targets, and the switch list has two targets beside the House stairs and two beside the Crypt, where the four gargoyles sit in the photo.
  • The rulesheet's Trunk targets (36-24-36) are the manual's Left 3-Bank, and its Hand of Fate "right target bank" is the manual's Right 3-Bank beside the HAND, OF and FATE inserts. The top lanes are printed DEEP and END; I read them as the rulesheet's Dead End lanes.
  • The Wild Market Value target is a guess: the manual has a For Sale/Wild Market lamp and a target switch near the For Sale sign right of the House.
  • The House hides the Back Door, the Cellar and the House Lock, the right ramp plastic covers the pop bumpers, and the Deadhead inside the Crypt cannot be seen. Those marks follow the manual. Slingshots are not marked.

Sources: Rulesheet, Stern, Stern LE/Premium manual (archived copy), Stern feature matrix. The photo is Stern's Premium playfield shot from its game page. The apron instruction card in that photo was also used.
