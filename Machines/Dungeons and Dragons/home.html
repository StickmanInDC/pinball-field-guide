Pick your machine above, then a rulesheet section. The artwork changes to match. The playfield highlights the shots for the step you are on, and the arrow keys move between steps.

The rulesheet treats Premium and LE as one game. It lists three Premium/LE playfield differences: Rath moves on two axes, Rath can eject balls during Dragon Multiball, and a pop-up scoop starts Dungeon Crawl. Each model has its own photo.

How far to trust the highlights
  • Rules come from the community rulesheet, based on code 1.11. Its Layout section is only two photos.
  • Shot positions come from Stern's Pro service manual: its lamp list names each pair of shot arrows (Left Orbit, Loop, Center Ramp, Spinner, Inner Loop, Right Ramp, Right Orbit) and its diagrams show where they sit. I matched the diagram to the photo by eye, so treat every lane as approximate.
  • Each highlighted feature carries a tag. Clear means a printed label, insert or toy identifies it in the photo. Estimated means I placed it from the manual or from context and it is worth confirming on the machine.
  • The rulesheet and the manual use different names. I read the rulesheet's side ramp as the manual's center ramp (the green Bridge ramp), the upper loop as the manual's Loop beside Rath, and the shortcut loop as the manual's Inner Loop. These are my readings.
  • The manual has a Left Ramp switch and an up/down ramp, but no left ramp arrows. I take the left ramp to start in the left orbit lane, which fits the rulesheet's "shoot the left orbit to lower the ramp". The rulesheet also names a "side loop" that I could not identify.
  • The Dragon targets sit under Rath's head and cannot be seen from above; they are marked from the manual. The hidden temple and secret door are also placed from the manual.
  • On the Premium and LE, Rath sits a little differently in the photo. The pop-up scoop is marked at the dungeon panel in front of the center lane, which is my guess.

Sources: Rulesheet, Stern, Stern Pro manual. The apron instruction card in Stern's photo was also used.
