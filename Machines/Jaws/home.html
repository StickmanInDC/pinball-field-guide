Pick your machine above, then a rulesheet section. The artwork changes to match. The playfield highlights the shots for the step you are on, and the arrow keys move between steps.

The rulesheet treats Premium and LE as one game and marks the Orca upper playfield, Orca Wheel Awards and Say Ah! as Premium and LE only. Each model has its own photo.

How far to trust the highlights
  • Rules come from the supplied rulesheet, based on code 1.01. Its Layout section is only two photos, so the shot names on the playfield are my reading of the inserts.
  • Each highlighted feature carries a tag. Clear means a printed label, insert or toy identifies it in the photo. Estimated means I placed it from context and it is worth confirming on the machine.
  • Five white shark arrows each carry a mode insert, and the rulesheet starts each mode at a named shot. That fixes the five beach shots: left orbit (Night Swim), wave ramp (Beach Panic), center ramp (Scars), fishing reel (Pond Attack) and right ramp (Raft Attack). The paths past the arrows are estimated.
  • The rulesheet starts Night Swim at the "left spinner" but lights the "left orbit" elsewhere. I treat them as one lane, which it does not state.
  • The harpoon lane is my reading of the Harpoon and Quickshot arrows on the left. The shark fin is marked along the curved slot it appears to travel in. The lanes above the mini-flipper, the left target bank and the pop bumper are also estimated.
  • Slingshots and spinner blades are not marked.
  • On the Pro, the left target bank is standup targets, and Stern lists a decorative shark breaking through a shark cage behind the center captive ball. The rulesheet's shark hits on the Pro go to the captive ball.
  • On the Premium and LE, the left target bank is drop targets, and a motorized shark crashes up through Ben Gardner's boat, per Stern. The Orca upper playfield, its steering wheel spinner, Lookout Tower ramp and radio target are marked from the photos. The Lookout Tower ramp path is a guess.

Sources: Rulesheet, Stern Pro, Stern Premium.
