Pick your machine above, then a rulesheet section. The artwork and the rules change to match. The playfield highlights the shots for the step you are on, and the arrow keys move between steps.

The rulesheet treats Premium and LE as one game and there is one photo for both, so the LE view matches Premium.

How far to trust the highlights
  • Rules come from the supplied rulesheet (code 1.08). It separates Pro from Premium/LE only in a few places, and each is shown here under the version it applies to.
  • The rulesheet says the portal starts Gem Quests on Premium/LE, but says "right ramp" for most other starts on every model. I kept its wording. Check the lit shot on a Premium or LE.
  • I placed the portal at the disc because one retailer lists a motorized pop-up disc scoop on these models. The rulesheet does not say where the portal is.
  • Each highlighted feature carries a tag. Clear means a label or toy identifies it in the photo. Estimated means I placed it from context and it is worth confirming on the machine.
  • Most shot paths are Estimated. I worked them out from the arrow inserts and character art, and found no source that maps them.
  • The Ant-Man shot and Bullseye targets were located by the site owner, not from a published source. I still can't place the rulesheet's "inner loop". Slingshots are not highlighted.
