The rulesheet treats the models as one game, except that the two Electric Magic multiballs are Premium and LE only. Stern's manuals show hardware differences too: the Pro's side shot is the Hermit target, while the Premium and LE have a side ramp there and the Electric Magic device in the middle. So the Pro and Premium each have their own photo. The LE uses the Premium photo.

How far to trust the highlights
  • Rules come from the community rulesheet, based on code 1.20. Its Layout section is two photos with no text.
  • Shot positions come from Stern's service manuals. Their lamp lists name an arrow for each of the seven major shots (Left Eject, Left Orbit, Left Ramp, Hermit Target or Side Ramp, Center Ramp, Right Ramp, Right Orbit) and their diagrams show where the switches and lamps sit. I matched the diagrams to the photo by eye, so treat every lane as approximate.
  • Each highlighted feature carries a tag. Clear means a printed label, insert or toy identifies it in the photo. Estimated means I placed it from the manual or from context and it is worth confirming on the machine.
  • The rulesheet's "side shot", "side flipper shot" and "upper flipper shot" I read as one shot: the Hermit target on the Pro, the side ramp on the Premium and LE, made from the upper right flipper. That is my reading.
  • The Icarus target, the top eject, the left spinner, the ROCK bank and the left two-bank are hard to see from above; they are placed from the manual.
  • The Premium photo was warped to line up with the Pro photo so both share one set of marks. Its center art differs from the Pro (band portraits and the Electric Magic panel), so marks there follow the Premium hardware.
  • The LE has the Premium's playfield layout. Stern's LE photo shows blue armor and a signature on the playfield; those are trim, so the LE uses the Premium photo.

Sources: Rulesheet, Stern Pro manual, Stern Premium/LE manual. The photos are Stern's own top views, linked from the rulesheet (Pro, Premium).
