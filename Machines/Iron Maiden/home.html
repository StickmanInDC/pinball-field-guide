Pick your machine above, then a rulesheet section. The artwork changes to match. The playfield highlights the shots for the step you are on, and the arrow keys move between steps.

The rulesheet treats Premium and LE as one game, so they share rules and art. Steps that differ from the Pro show only on the model they apply to.

How far to trust the highlights
  • Rules come from the supplied rulesheet, based on code 1.10.
  • Each highlighted feature carries a tag. Clear means a label or toy identifies it in the photo. Estimated means I placed it from context and it is worth confirming on the machine.
  • Printed inserts are reliable. Almost every shot path is not. Ramps and wireforms cross over much of the upper playfield, and the rulesheet has no layout text, only photos. I matched shots to arrows using the Power inserts beside them, and that match is my reading, not a stated fact.
  • The left ramp, the two upper loops, both spinners, the Orb and the Super Jackpot target are the placements I am least sure of. The skill shot and gravestone targets were located by the machine's owner.
  • The rulesheet says "right loop" for the Pro Tomb and a Trooper lock, but never defines it. Those steps mark the right orbit as a stand-in.
  • The Underworld Scoop position is a guess. The rulesheet only says it is under the center ramp.
  • I used Stern's Premium photo for the LE too. Stern's site has no LE page for this game.

Sources: Rulesheet, Stern.
