How far to trust the highlights
  • Rules come from the supplied rulesheet, based on code 0.87. It has no layout text, only photos.
  • Each highlighted feature carries a tag. Clear means a printed label or toy identifies it in the photo. Estimated means I placed it from context and it is worth confirming on the machine.
  • The rulesheet gives the left to right order of the four main shots. I matched them to the four character lanes. Their arrows are clear, but clear ramps and wires hide most of the paths.
  • I could not see the spinner, bumpers, berry targets, town targets or the Psyduck target and scoop. Those marks sit at the nearest insert or art, or the likely area.
  • The rulesheet says Battlefield and BATTLE targets, and town, right and plain scoop. I treat each group as one feature. It says town targets where the apron card says Chansey targets, and I treat those as one set too.
  • The center berry target is my reading from left to right order. The Center Playfield X Target in the Jolteon combo is not marked, as I could not place it.
  • The rulesheet does not cover Pokémon Master. That step comes from Stern's apron card, read from a blurry photo.
