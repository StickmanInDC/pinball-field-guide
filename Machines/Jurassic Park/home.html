Pick your machine above, then a rulesheet section. The artwork changes to match. The playfield highlights the shots for the step you are on, and the arrow keys move between steps.

The rulesheet treats Premium and LE as one game, so they share rules and art. Steps that differ from the Pro show only on the model they apply to.

How far to trust the highlights
  • Rules come from the supplied rulesheet, based on code 1.10. It has no layout text, only photos.
  • Each highlighted feature carries a tag. Clear means a printed label or toy identifies it in the photo. Estimated means I placed it from context and it is worth confirming on the machine.
  • I matched the eight white arrows to shots from their positions, printed labels and the wire returns. That match is my reading, not a stated fact. The upper loop and side ramp arrows are the least certain and may be swapped.
  • The rulesheet never says which shots carry the CHAOS letters. I read C as the Control Room lane, H as the left ramp, A as the right ramp, O as the right orbit and S as the helipad ramp, from the letter below each arrow.
  • I could not see the spinner, the Light Escape standup, the Supply Drop target, the Light 2x Scoring standup or the Set Trap targets themselves. Those steps mark the printed insert or lane nearest to where the rulesheet puts them.
  • The rulesheet says "side ramp" and "tower ramp", and "upper loop" and "loop". I treat each pair as one shot. It also calls the pops standup the amber target, and I treat those as one.
  • I used Stern's Premium photo for the LE too. Stern's site has no LE page for this game.

Sources: Rulesheet, Stern.
