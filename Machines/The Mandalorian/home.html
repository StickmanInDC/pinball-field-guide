Pick your machine above, then a rulesheet section. The artwork changes to match. The playfield highlights the shots for the step you are on, and the arrow keys move between steps.

The rulesheet treats the three models as one game, with a few notes. The Encounter mini playfield is a fixed one-flipper playfield on the Pro and a tilting two-flipper playfield on the Premium and LE, the Rising Phoenix on the Pro is the Jet Pack on the Premium and LE, and Ambush is set up at the U-turn on the Pro and the right scoop on the Premium and LE. Stern's features list shows more hardware differences, so each model has its own photo.

How far to trust the highlights
  • Rules come from the community rulesheet, which is based on code 1.42. Its Layout section is two photos with no text.
  • Shot positions come from Stern's Pro service manual. Its lamp list names an arrow for each shot (Left Orbit, Left Eject, Left Ramp, Razor, Right Scoop, Right Ramp, Right Orbit) and its diagrams show where the switches sit. I matched the diagrams to the photo by eye, so treat every lane as approximate.
  • Each highlighted feature carries a tag. Clear means a printed label, insert or toy identifies it in the photo. Estimated means I placed it from the manual or from context and it is worth confirming on the machine.
  • Names are my readings. The rulesheet's U-turn is the shot Stern's Pro lamp list calls Right Scoop and its features list calls the inner loop under the right helix ramp. The rulesheet's center ramp is the Razor arrow under the Razor Crest ship. The BOBA and FETT targets are the manual's Right 2 Bank, and the 2x targets are its Right Ramp Left and Right Targets.
  • The rulesheet calls the U-turn, left ramp, center ramp, right ramp and both orbits the "major shots" only by implication. I treat those six as the major shots and the left scoop as the finishing shot.
  • The Razor Crest target hangs under the ship and cannot be seen from above. The pop bumpers, the 2x targets, the BOBA and FETT targets and the Hunter targets are also hard to see; they are placed from the manual.
  • On the Premium and LE the mini playfield sits higher up, under the large helmet, with two flippers. Grogu's "Force" magnet and the up/down horseshoe scoop at the U-turn are Premium and LE features in Stern's features list; the rulesheet does not describe the magnet. The LE photo shows the same layout as the Premium with different trim.
