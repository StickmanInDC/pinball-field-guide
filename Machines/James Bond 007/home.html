Pick your machine above, then a rulesheet section. The artwork changes to match. The playfield highlights the shots for the step you are on, and the arrow keys move between steps.

The rulesheet treats Premium and LE as one game, so they share rules and art. Steps that differ from the Pro show only on the model they apply to.

How far to trust the highlights
  • Rules come from the supplied rulesheet, based on code 1.01.
  • Each highlighted feature carries a tag. Clear means a printed label or toy identifies it in the photo. Estimated means I placed it from context and it is worth confirming on the machine.
  • The rulesheet has no layout text, only photos. I matched shots to arrows from printed names, nearby inserts and what the rulesheet says about each shot. That match is my reading, not a stated fact.
  • The left ramp is the placement I am least sure of. I could not see where it starts, and it may be swapped with the rocket lane. The left eject, DB5 eject, spinner, bumper lane and Goldfinger target are also worth checking.
  • The rulesheet says "upper loop" and "side loop", and "center lane", "center loop" and "center orbit". I treat each set as one shot, which it does not state. I also treat "left eject" and "left scoop" as one.
  • The apron card says the left orbit starts Jetpack features. The rulesheet says the left eject, and this guide follows the rulesheet.
  • The rulesheet refers to "nine major shots" without listing them. Steps that use them mark the ten main shots I identified.
  • The Bird One lock ring and jetpack track are marked from the toys in the photo. The rulesheet does not describe their paths.
  • I used Stern's Premium photo for the LE too. Stern's site has no LE page for this game.
