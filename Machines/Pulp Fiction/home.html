Pick a rulesheet section. Each one is a short run of steps, and the playfield highlights the shots for the step you are on. Use the arrow keys to move between steps.

Chicago Gaming Company sells a Special Edition, a Special Edition with a bill validator (SED) and the Bad Mother Flipper Limited Edition. Its feature matrix lists every playfield feature, toy and mechanism for all models; the LE adds a topper, speaker grill, shaker motor, mirror blades and a wallet plaque. This guide shows one playfield for all of them.

The rulesheet gives no code revision. Its Layout section is blank. Chicago Gaming's own Rules Manual (release 1.7, February 2024) is used here to name the shots, and where the two disagree the guide says so instead of picking one.

How far to trust the highlights
  • The picture is not a photo. It is the top-down layout drawing from Chicago Gaming's Rules Manual, with the manual's shot arrows and labels removed. Ramps, plastics and toys are drawn flat and partly see-through, so it is a map of the machine rather than a picture of one.
  • Each highlighted feature carries a tag. Clear means a printed label or insert identifies it, or the rules manual outlines it on its own shot map. Estimated means I placed it from context and it is worth confirming on the machine.
  • The saucers, the briefcase lane and its drop targets, the pawn shop scoop and center drop targets, the Roll Scene lane, saucer and drop target, the orbits, the seven red arrow targets, the Big Kahuna targets, the Royale with Cheese target, the bullseye target, the MPH Bonus target, the lanes and the inserts are clear.
  • The spinners, the bullseye magnet, the rotating briefcase, the ball release rail, the flippers and the plunger lane are estimated.

Sources: Rulesheet, Chicago Gaming Company, Rules Manual.
