Pick your machine above, then a rulesheet section. The artwork changes to match. The playfield highlights the shots for the step you are on, and the arrow keys move between steps.

The rulesheet treats Premium and LE as one game, so they share rules and art. Steps that differ from the Pro show only on the model they apply to.

How far to trust the highlights
  • Rules come from the supplied rulesheet, based on code 1.04.
  • Each highlighted feature carries a tag. Clear means a label, toy or Stern photo caption identifies it. Estimated means I placed it from context and it is worth confirming on the machine.
  • The rulesheet has no layout text, only photos. I matched shots to arrows using the band member portraits and the inserts beside them, and that match is my reading, not a stated fact.
  • The side ramp and side loop are the placements I am least sure of. The two arrows could be swapped. The left crossover, right orbit, Rock-O-Meter lane and Lightning target are also guesses. The Bot Award target is not marked, because its location is not confirmed.
  • Where a shot runs behind metal or wireforms, I marked only the part I could follow.
  • The Area 51 upper playfield shots are placed from their arrows and inserts. The owner confirmed that the left arrow is the spinner and the right arrow is the loop.
  • I used Stern's Premium photo for the LE too. Stern's site has no LE page for this game.

Sources: Rulesheet, Stern.
