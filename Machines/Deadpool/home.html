The rulesheet treats Premium and LE as one game and marks Disco Loops as Premium and LE only. Each model has its own photo.

How far to trust the highlights
  • Rules come from the supplied rulesheet, based on code 1.10. Its Layout section is only two photos, so the shot order is my reading of the playfield.
  • Each highlighted feature carries a tag. Clear means a printed label, insert or toy identifies it in the photo. Estimated means I placed it from context and it is worth confirming on the machine.
  • Stern's apron card is readable on the photos. It calls the left spinner the Dazzler spinner and the U-turn the Colossus Loop. Stern's features list adds a "Hell House" scoop, two spinners (Dazzler and Wolverine), a katana ramp return with the multiball lock in its handle, and pop bumpers with shuriken caps.
  • The left ramp and U-turn are the placements I am least sure of. I read the lower arrow of the second lane as the left ramp and the upper arrow as the U-turn, and they may be the other way round. The scoop, left orbit path, right orbit path and target banks are also worth checking.
  • The owner confirmed three spots: the SNIKT! target sits right of the center spinner on the left side of the pop bumpers, the Dazzler spinner is in the left orbit, and the Colossal Jackpot target is reached through the Colossus Loop. The SNIKT! target and Dazzler spinner are marked where I read them in the photo. The Colossal Jackpot target is not visible, so steps mark the U-turn. Stern's Pro page says SNIKT! redirects up the left ramp, but the guide follows the rulesheet's right ramp. Slingshots are not marked.
  • The rulesheet says "center spinner" and "center loop". I treat them as one shot, which it does not state.
  • On the Pro, D-E-A-D and P-O-O-L are standup targets. The Premium and LE have drop targets in the same places.
  • On the Premium and LE, D-E-A-D and P-O-O-L are drop targets, per Stern's features list. The disco ball, Wolverine and Dazzler figures are marked from the photos.

Sources: Rulesheet, Stern, Stern features list.
