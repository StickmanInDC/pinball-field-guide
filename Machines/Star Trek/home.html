Pick your machine above, then a rulesheet section. The artwork changes to match. The playfield highlights the shots for the step you are on, and the arrow keys move between steps.

The rulesheet does not separate the models, so they share rules. Pro and Premium have their own photos. Stern publishes no full LE photo, so LE shows the Premium one.

How far to trust the highlights
  • Rules come from the supplied rulesheet, based on code 1.62. It has no layout text, only photos. Its Five Year Mission text stops mid-sentence.
  • Each highlighted feature carries a tag. Clear means a printed label, insert or toy identifies it in the photo. Estimated means I placed it from context and it is worth confirming on the machine.
  • Stern's apron card is readable in the Premium photo. It names the Klingon, Power and Weapons, Shields and Red Matter targets, and I used it to place them. Lines taken from the card say so.
  • The order of the seven main shots comes from the rulesheet's Klingon Battle II pattern. The arrows and printed Orbit labels fit that order, but clear ramps hide most of the paths past the entrances.
  • The Vengeance standups, the magnet, the pop bumpers and the slingshots are not marked, because I could not place them. The Black Hole target is my reading of what its purple arrow points at.

Sources: Rulesheet, Stern.
