Pick your machine above, then a rulesheet section. The artwork changes to match. The playfield highlights the shots for the step you are on, and the arrow keys move between steps.

The rulesheet treats Premium and LE as one game. It gives them a different skill shot, Catapult Multiball on an upper playfield, a left drop target in place of a spinner, and a Last Chance feature it does not describe. Each model has its own photo.

How far to trust the highlights
  • Rules come from the community rulesheet, based on code 1.14. Its Layout section is only two photos.
  • Shot positions come from Stern's service manuals. Their lamp lists name a lightning bolt insert for each shot (Castle, Left Orbit, Flail, Center Target, Shield, Shield Target, Right Orbit) and their diagrams show where the switches sit. I matched the diagrams to the photo by eye, so treat every lane as approximate.
  • Each highlighted feature carries a tag. Clear means a printed label, insert or toy identifies it in the photo. Estimated means I placed it from the manual or from context and it is worth confirming on the machine.
  • The rulesheet and the manual use different names. I read the rulesheet's "left spinner lane" as the manual's far left Castle lane, because the Knight Mode and Add-A-Ball inserts sit in that lane and the apron card says KNIGHT Mode starts at the Castle VUK. I read the "light lock" target as the manual's Shield Target, the "ramp" as the Flail Ramp, and the "saucer behind the shield" as the Shield VUK. These are my readings.
  • The rulesheet says Super Modes are lit by "right spinner spins", but both manuals list only left side spinners. Super Mode steps mark the spinners as unconfirmed.
  • The pop bumper under the Knight's plume, the add time targets and the drop targets are hard to see from above; they are placed from the manual.
  • On the Premium and LE, the upper playfield and its catapult lock sit at the top left. The lock target beside the catapult is not visible in the photo, so its mark is my guess.

Sources: Rulesheet, Stern, Stern Pro manual, Stern Premium/LE manual. The photos are Stern's, linked from the rulesheet. The apron instruction card in Stern's Pro photo was also used.
