The rulesheet treats Premium and LE as one game. It marks the car skill shot, the physical Deconsecrated locks, swipe-a-ball and the Excommunicado short-plunge locks as Premium and LE only, and gives the Premium and LE different car hit counts. Each model has its own photo.

How far to trust the highlights
  • Rules come from the supplied rulesheet, based on code 1.02. It has no Layout section, so the shot names on the playfield are my reading of the inserts and of Stern's feature list.
  • Each highlighted feature carries a tag. Clear means a printed label, insert or toy identifies it in the photo. Estimated means I placed it from context and it is worth confirming on the machine.
  • The rulesheet names the allies' shots: Akira at the left ramp, Charon at the center ramp, Katia at the right ramp. Matching diamond inserts sit at three arrows, and Stern names the "Katia" right ramp. The Dance Club insert and red lane lead to the Red Circle bumper. Those four shots are the most certain. Paths past the entrances are my reading.
  • The left eject is placed from a small "Administration" label on the left. The left orbit is the lane beside it. The right orbit is my reading of the narrow lane left of the Katia ramp. Its enemy insert sits higher up, beside the Continental, and I am assuming it belongs to that lane.
  • The orange standup targets, the gold coin targets, the pop bumper by the crate, the right VUK and the blood marker's standup targets are placed from context. The left orbit spinner, the VIP lane, the slingshots and the car skill shot lane are not marked.
  • On the Pro, the rulesheet locks Deconsecrated Multiball balls virtually and requires a spinner rip before the car can be lit.
  • On the Premium and LE, Stern lists a motorized Blood Oath Marker and a drop target entrance to the Red Circle Club. The rulesheet says the car can be hit from several angles and locks the ball during Car Chase Multiball, and that Deconsecrated locks are physical at the center ramp.
