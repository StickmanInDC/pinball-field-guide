How far to trust the highlights
  • Rules come from the supplied rulesheet (code 0.96). The game is new and the sheet is unfinished: The Ultimate Doom is "still assembling", Prime Target's Energized rule is blank, and two One Shall Fall values are "???".
  • Each highlighted feature carries a tag. Clear means a label or toy identifies it in the photo. Estimated means I placed it from context and it is worth confirming on the machine.
  • Most shot paths are Estimated. I worked them out by matching the rulesheet to the inserts along each lane, such as Add-A-Ball and Extra Ball for the space bridge, and found no source that maps them. The wire ramps hide much of the routing.
  • The Grimlock and Soundwave models on this photo hide the Grimlock targets and the upper flipper, so those spots are placed from the Pro photo.
  • The rulesheet names a Cassette target and a Soundwave target. I treat them as one, which it does not state. The tech spec standups are my reading of the small targets in the photo. Slingshots are marked at their lit inserts.

Sources: Rulesheet, Stern.
