Pick your machine above, then a rulesheet section. The artwork changes to match. The playfield highlights the shots for the step you are on, and the arrow keys move between steps.

The rulesheet treats Premium and LE as one game. It gives the Pro no gong, so island scenes light from blue island arrows, swaps which KING KONG letter lights Deadeye, and has the Pro light King Kong Multiball at the right orbit with no physical train lock. Each model has its own photo.

The rulesheet is for code 0.97 and is unfinished. Terror on the Tower and the 8th Wonder wizard mode say only "TBD", and several values are question marks. The guide marks those gaps instead of filling them in.

How far to trust the highlights
  • Rules come from the supplied rulesheet, based on code 0.97. Its Layout section is blank, so the shot names on the playfield are my reading of the inserts and of Stern's feature list.
  • Each highlighted feature carries a tag. Clear means a printed label, insert or toy identifies it in the photo. Estimated means I placed it from context and it is worth confirming on the machine.
  • The center ramp is fixed by its train station Lock inserts, and the gong shot by the Island Scene insert and the gong. Kong, the biplane, the pit magnet, the KING KONG letters, the building and the gate inserts are clear.
  • The far left lanes are my reading: the cliffs ramp under the Light Island Jackpot insert, the river beside the Log Bridge insert, and the left orbit next to them. The side ramp, center spinner, U-turn, right orbit and Kong Cave lane are also my reading.
  • The KONG drop targets, Island Treasure target, power-up target, pit targets and punchback target are placed from the rulesheet's descriptions; I could not see them in the photos. The spinners, slingshots, orange 2x targets and the Pro's blue island arrows are not marked.
  • On the Pro, King Kong is static, there is no gong toy, and the gong art is printed where the Premium gong stands. The rulesheet says the Pro does not hold the ball for King Kong relocks.
  • On the Premium and LE, Stern lists the sculpted gong with Kong's VUK behind it, an animatronic Kong that flips the elevated train car lock, and a giant spider over the pit.
