Pick your machine above, then a rulesheet section. The artwork changes to match. The playfield highlights the shots for the step you are on, and the arrow keys move between steps.

The rulesheet treats Premium and LE as one game, so they share rules and art. Steps that differ from the Pro show only on the model they apply to.

How far to trust the highlights
  • Rules come from the supplied rulesheet, based on code 1.16.
  • Each highlighted feature carries a tag. Clear means a printed label or toy identifies it in the photo. Estimated means I placed it from context and it is worth confirming on the machine.
  • The rulesheet has no layout text, only photos. I matched shots to arrows by lining up the TANK inserts, printed names and the left-to-right order of the Monster Rampage shots. That match is my reading, not a stated fact.
  • The upper loop, tail whip lane, right spinner, left ramp path, Magna-Grab captive ball, powerline targets and Maser Cannon target are the placements I am least sure of. Wireforms hide much of the upper playfield, so I marked only what I could follow.
  • The rulesheet says "upper loop" and "big loop". I treat them as one shot, which it does not state.
  • Not marked, because I could not find them: the reverse building exit (Wallop), the Mecha side lane and Mecha bottom rollover.
  • I could not pick out the two Pro Neo Barrier standups, so those steps mark the printed Neo Barrier inserts.
  • The Neo Barrier target bank is marked on the rotating drum beside the Mechagodzilla toy. Its exact position, and the jump ramp, are guesses.
  • I used Stern's Premium photo for the LE too. Stern's site has no LE page for this game.

Sources: Rulesheet, Stern.
