Barrels of Fun · Dune · 2025 · Code Rev 2026.03.30

Dune

Pick a rulesheet section. Each one is a short run of steps, and the playfield highlights the shots for the step you are on. Use the arrow keys to move between steps.

Barrels of Fun's press release describes one Dune model, "limited up to 1,000 units worldwide". So this guide shows one playfield.

The rulesheet is based on code revision 2026.03.30. Its layout section is only a photo, and I could not find a Barrels of Fun game manual or rules map for Dune. So most shots here are my placement from the photo, guided by Barrels of Fun's flyer callouts and by how the rulesheet groups the shots. Where the rulesheet is silent the guide says "not documented yet".

How far to trust the highlights
  • The picture is Barrels of Fun's own overhead photo of the playfield (the larger copy of the one the rulesheet links). It was taken from above the front of the machine, so the back is farther from the camera. I corrected the perspective from the cabinet's outline. Ramps, wireforms and the sculpts stand up off the playfield, so they lean in the result, and the back of the playfield is softer than the front.
  • Each highlighted feature carries a tag. Clear means a printed label, Barrels of Fun's flyer or an obvious part identifies it in the photo. Estimated means I placed it from context and it is worth confirming on the machine. Most shots are estimated.
  • Clear: the flippers and slingshots, the upper flipper, the three Fremkit drop targets, the Sand Trap lane, the Desert Power field, the sandworm, Harvester and Sietch mountain sculpts, the prophecy mode inserts and the DUNE mode inserts.
  • Estimated: every orbit, ramp and lane shot (left spinner, left ramp, left orbit, worm, center, tunnel, right ramp, side ramp, right orbit), the scoop, the Thumper bumper, the standup targets, the Sand Trap drop target, the Harvester and DUNE letter inserts, The Voice and Pain Box inserts, the in and outlanes and the shooter lane. Which left lane is the left spinner and which is the left orbit, and which right lane is the side ramp, are my guesses.

Sources: Rulesheet, Barrels of Fun's Dune flyer (feature callouts), Barrels of Fun's press release (feature list, production run), the overhead playfield photo and rear playfield photo from Barrels of Fun's site, and its scoop protector instructions (which show the scoop beside the upper flipper).
