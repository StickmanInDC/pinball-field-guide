Halloween

Pick a rulesheet section. Each one is a short run of steps, and the playfield highlights the shots for the step you are on. Use the arrow keys to move between steps.

Spooky Pinball sold Halloween in three editions: Standard, Blood Sucker and Collector's. Spooky's announcement lists the same playfield features for all three; the Blood Sucker and Collector's Editions add trim (powder coat, plastic protectors, speaker kit, knocker and shaker, and on the Collector's Edition a topper and extra decorative toy). So this guide shows one playfield.

The rulesheet gives no code revision, and its Rules Overview, Layout and End-of-Ball Bonus sections are blank. Spooky's release notes (latest v1.19) add features the rulesheet does not cover, such as Stalker Survival. This guide says "not documented yet" where the rulesheet is silent.

How far to trust the highlights
  • The picture is a promo photo of a finished machine (a Collector's Edition, by its orange plastic protectors) from the Halloween product page of Pinball Heaven, a UK Spooky distributor. It was taken from the front at a slight angle, so I corrected the perspective to make the playfield look straight down. The ramps, the hedges, the house and other parts that stand up off the playfield lean in the result, and the top of the playfield is stretched a little.
  • Each highlighted feature carries a tag. Clear means a printed label, insert or toy identifies it in the picture. Estimated means I placed it from context and it is worth confirming on the machine.
  • The rulesheet has no layout section. Shots were placed from the photo and from Spooky's service manual, which has a numbered switch location photo (of Ultraman, which shares the Halloween layout), a switch chart and a numbered LED location photo of a bare Halloween playfield.
  • The hedges, the Jack-O-Lantern drop bank and insert, the pumpkin scoop, the Judith Myers tombstone, the middle (Sanitarium) playfield, the house playfield, the mode inserts, the slingshots and the lower flippers are clear. The orbits, the ramps and their paths, the hedge scoops and standups, the top standups, the Judith drop target and the target behind it, the middle playfield targets and scoop, the house spinners, drop target and crossover, the lanes and the shooter lane are estimated.

Sources: Rulesheet, Spooky's Halloween and Ultraman service manual, Spooky's Halloween support page (release notes), This Week in Pinball's announcement overview (edition features) and the playfield photo (Pinball Heaven).
