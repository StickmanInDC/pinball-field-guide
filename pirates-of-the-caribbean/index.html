Pirates of the Caribbean

Pick a rulesheet section. Each one is a short run of steps, and the playfield highlights the shots for the step you are on. Use the arrow keys to move between steps.

Jersey Jack Pinball sold Pirates of the Caribbean as a Standard, Limited and Collector's Edition. JJP's model comparison sheet lists differences in trim, glass, lighting, art, topper and extras. It lists "molded playfield toys" and "pop bumper rotation and toys" for the Limited and Collector's only, and the game manual marks the spinning pop bumper sculptures as LE/CE parts. It does not list a different shot layout or different rules, so this guide shows one playfield for all three.

The rulesheet is marked work in progress and gives no code revision. Shot positions come from the shot maps in JJP's game manual (November 2018), and shot names follow the rulesheet. Where the rulesheet and the manual disagree the guide says so. Where the rulesheet is blank the guide says "not documented yet".

How far to trust the highlights
  • The picture is Jersey Jack Pinball's own straight-down photo of the playfield (POTC-playfield-full.jpg in its distributor photo pack), cropped at the apron. JJP does not say which edition it shows; the figure on the pop bumpers suggests a Limited or Collector's Edition. The top edge cuts through the back of the playfield, so the target ship (The Dauntless) across the back is not in the picture, and the Black Pearl ship hides the main playfield under it.
  • Each highlighted feature carries a tag. Clear means a printed label, an insert or an obvious part identifies it in the photo. Estimated means I placed it from the manual's shot maps and it is worth confirming on the machine. I lined the manual's layout drawing up with the photo on the inserts, flippers and spinning disc; it matches closely on the lower and middle playfield.
  • Clear: the Maelstrom ramp and whirlpool, the upper left flipper, the Black Pearl ramp and ship, the treasure chest, the chest lane and Star Map arrows, the Port Royal and Devil's Triangle arrows, the Map Ready insert, the spinning disc, the PIRATE lane inserts, the wizard mode inserts, the two flasher domes and the shooter lane.
  • Estimated: the orbit and loop paths, the Depths, the Bayou, the Captain's Quarters (MAP) hole, the Star Map targets, the PIRATE, Chest, MAP, GOLD and X targets, the pop bumpers and their rollover, the Tortuga hole, the right lane, the up-post, the ship popper, the cannon, the Spot Character target and the smaller inserts.

Sources: Rulesheet (work in progress), JJP's game manual (section A.2, rules and shot maps; parts lists in section C), JJP's model comparison sheet, and the playfield photo from JJP's distributor folder (inside Pirates of the Caribbean.zip) via Jersey Jack Pinball support.
