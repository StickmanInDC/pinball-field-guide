Sonic the Hedgehog

Pick a rulesheet section. Each one is a short run of steps, and the playfield highlights the shots for the step you are on. Use the arrow keys to move between steps.

Jersey Jack Pinball sells Sonic the Hedgehog as an Arcade Edition, a Special Edition and a Collector's Edition. JJP's flyer lists the same game features for all three and differs only in artwork, armor, toppers, glass, lighting and accessories, and JJP's straight-down photos of the three playfields show the same layout. So this guide shows one playfield for every edition.

The rulesheet is written for code 0.94, a game released in June 2026, and is still being filled in: one zone's shot map is empty, another is partial, and the bonus section is blank. Where it is silent the guide says "not documented yet". JJP's game manual has no shot maps, so shot positions come from the photo, JJP's close-up photos and the manual's opto and coil drawings. Where JJP's rules flow chart disagrees with the rulesheet the guide says so.

How far to trust the highlights
  • The picture is Jersey Jack Pinball's own straight-down photo of the Special Edition playfield, from its distributor photo folder, cropped below the flippers. No perspective correction was needed. The lab upper playfield, the ramps and the plastics stand above the playfield, so the battle zone and other parts under them are hidden or hard to see.
  • I lined the manual's opto drawing up with the photo using the zone inserts, the slingshots and the flippers. The drawing gives the left ramp, center ramp, right ramp, bash post and magnet loop switch positions, which I used to place hidden paths.
  • Each highlighted feature carries a tag. Clear means a printed label, an insert or an obvious part identifies it in the photo. Estimated means I placed it from the manual's drawings, JJP's close-ups or context and it is worth confirming on the machine.
  • Clear: the left orbit and left ramp entrances, the lockup lane, the loop, the Froggy target, the lab and its flippers, Eggman's mech, the HERO lanes, and the inserts.
  • Estimated: the center ramp and right ramp paths, the right orbit, the Bash and Chao targets, the inline drop targets, the center drop target, the six Badnik targets, and every lab shot. The lab loopback and jump ramps could not be traced at all; their highlight marks a general area only.

Sources: Rulesheet, JJP's game manual (opto maps, coil map and switch tables), JJP's rules flow chart, JJP's code notes, the flyers, and the Special Edition playfield photo and close-ups from Jersey Jack Pinball.
