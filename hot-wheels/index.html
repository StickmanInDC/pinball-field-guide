Hot Wheels

Pick a rulesheet section. Each one is a short run of steps, and the playfield highlights the shots for the step you are on. Use the arrow keys to move between steps.

American Pinball sold Hot Wheels as a Classic and a Deluxe edition, both now marked retired. Its page lists the same playfield features for both; the Deluxe adds a shaker, knocker, Magic Glass and upgraded rails. This guide shows one playfield for both.

The rulesheet is marked work in progress and gives no code revision. Its Car Chaos section is unfinished, Treasure Hunts are marked as needing testing, and a few numbers carry question marks. Where it is silent the guide says "not documented yet". The rulesheet gives a June 2020 release; American Pinball's page says October 2021. The rules chapter in American Pinball's game manual is shorter and differs in places; where that matters the guide says so.

How far to trust the highlights
  • The picture is American Pinball's own near straight-down playfield photo from its Hot Wheels sell sheet (flyer), cropped to the playfield. On the flyer a city picture covers part of the lower left playfield. I filled that patch (the BATTLE targets, the left lanes and the left slingshot area) with the same photo from a distributor's smaller listing, scaled up and lined up by computer, so that patch is softer than the rest and may be a few pixels off.
  • I lined American Pinball's switch map from the game manual up with the photo (the RPM targets and the Build Track drop target fit within a few pixels) and used it to place switches the photo does not show.
  • Each highlighted feature carries a tag. Clear means a printed label, an insert or an obvious part identifies it in the photo. Estimated means I placed it from the switch map or from context and it is worth confirming on the machine.
  • Clear: the race position inserts and lane arrows, the RPM targets, the Build Track sign and drop target, the pop bumpers, the spinning car, the BATTLE targets, the EPIC targets, the tach, gear, hero car, battle and Epic inserts, the E-P-I-C lane inserts and the flippers.
  • Estimated: where every orbit, loop and ramp goes past its entrance, the VUK, the turbo kicker, both spinners, the four car targets, the Advance Bonus X target, the loop magnet, the right saucer and its drop target, the Pit Stop target, the skill shot rollovers, the blue return ramp and the plunger lane.

Sources: Rulesheet (work in progress), American Pinball, game manual (switch map and rules chapter), sell sheet (playfield photo, archived copy), and the patch from Coin Op Express.
