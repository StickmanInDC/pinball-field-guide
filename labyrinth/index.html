Barrels of Fun · Labyrinth · 2023 · Code Rev 2024-09-25

Jim Henson's Labyrinth

Pick a rulesheet section. Each one is a short run of steps, and the playfield highlights the shots for the step you are on. Use the arrow keys to move between steps.

Barrels of Fun's product page lists one Labyrinth model, limited to 1,100 games, with a choice of two backglasses. So this guide shows one playfield.

The rulesheet is based on code revision 2024-09-25 and has no layout section. Barrels of Fun's manual (release 1.3) has a shot map for each rule, and its official rules map card is also used here. Where they disagree with the rulesheet the guide says so instead of picking one, and it says "not documented yet" where all of them are silent.

How far to trust the highlights
  • The picture is Barrels of Fun's own promo photo of a finished machine, from the Labyrinth page of its shop. It was taken from the front at an angle, so I warped it to line up with the flat playfield artwork printed in the manual. The inserts and the lower playfield line up well. Ramps, wireforms, the upper flipper and the toys stand up off the playfield, so they lean and stretch in the result, and the top of the playfield is soft and partly hidden.
  • Each highlighted feature carries a tag. Clear means the manual marks it on one of its shot maps, or a printed insert identifies it. Estimated means I placed it from context and it is worth confirming on the machine.
  • The orbits, ramps, inner loop, horseshoe, Wiseman drop target, scoop and targets, the forks, the Humongous drop target, the Helping Hands scoop, the Ello lane, the Junk Lady target, the wall and fairy targets, the Friend inserts, the mode inserts and the flippers are placed from the manual. The manual's maps sit on the flat artwork, and the warp is least accurate near the top, so expect those marks to be a little off there.
  • The spinner, the scoop under the left ramp, the Village entry, the Wiseman scoop exit, the Change Mode target, the right pillar target, the upper flipper, the Sir Didymus post, the slingshots, the lanes and the shooter wireform are estimated.

Sources: Rulesheet, Barrels of Fun's Labyrinth manual (section 2, Game Rules and Shot Maps), Barrels of Fun's rules map, and the Labyrinth product page (feature list and playfield photo).
