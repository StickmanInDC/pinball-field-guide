# Stern playfield images

`stern-playfield-contact-sheet.jpg` shows large images found on Stern's Transformers model pages (November 2026). Those pages embed images from other games too. Each thumbnail is labeled with the start of its ID and its pixel size.

Full image URL: `https://site-assets.plasmic.app/<id>.webp`

The sheet is not a full catalog. It covers the images 2560 pixels tall or more, and it leaves out four 1707x2560 images and two 1248-1250 pixel wide ones. Stern's pages also hold many tall images 2500 pixels high or less. To look for more, read the `site-assets.plasmic.app` links from a game's model page, such as `https://www.sternpinball.com/game/transformers-more-than-meets-the-eye/pro/`.

Game names are not given by Stern's filenames. Confirm the game and model by opening the image, and by its caption on the model page.
