Pick a rulesheet section. Each one is a short run of steps, and the playfield on the right highlights the shots for the step you are on. Use the arrow keys to move between steps.

How far to trust the highlights
  • Rules come from the Pinball Rule Sheets wiki page (code 0.97). Its Layout section and its Jedi Master section are both blank, so shot positions are my reading of your photo, checked against shot names in the Kineticist tutorial and review and the Retro Refurbs reveal.
  • Each highlighted feature carries a tag. Clear means a label or toy identifies it in the photo. Estimated means I placed it from context and it is worth confirming on the machine.
  • I can't tell which edition the photo shows. Where Pro and Prem/LE differ, the step says so.
  • Spinners and slingshots are described in the steps but not highlighted, because I could not locate them in the photo.

Sources: Pinball Rule Sheets, Kineticist tutorial, Kineticist review, Retro Refurbs.
