Dialed In!

Pick a rulesheet section. Each one is a short run of steps, and the playfield highlights the shots for the step you are on. Use the arrow keys to move between steps.

Jersey Jack Pinball sold Dialed In! as a Standard, Limited and Collector's Edition. JJP's model comparison sheet lists differences in trim, cabinet armor, ramp lighting, the theater emblem and extras. It does not list a different playfield layout, so this guide shows one playfield for all three. The sheet also lists "customized software" for the Collector's Edition without saying what it changes.

The rulesheet gives no code revision and has no layout section, and it calls itself "likely filled with errors". Shot positions come from the shot maps in JJP's game manual (November 2018), and shot names follow the rulesheet. Where the rulesheet and the manual disagree the guide says so. Where the rulesheet is silent the guide says "not documented yet".

How far to trust the highlights
  • The picture is Jersey Jack Pinball's own overhead photo of the Limited Edition playfield, from its distributor photo folder. It was taken from above the front of the machine, so I corrected the perspective from the cabinet's side rails. Ramps, wireforms, Betty and the drones stand up off the playfield, so they lean in the result.
  • Each highlighted feature carries a tag. Clear means a printed label, an insert or an obvious part identifies it in the photo. Estimated means I placed it from the manual's shot maps or from context and it is worth confirming on the machine.
  • Clear: the Quantum Theater, the smartphone, the QED character, Betty, the B-O-B targets and letters, the upper right flipper, the TIC and KET inserts, the Package Delivery inserts, the D-I-A-L-E-D I-N inserts, the in and outlanes, the kickback and the shooter lane.
  • Estimated: every ramp and orbit path, the smartphone scoop, the SIM Card scoop, the skill shot kicker, Crazy Bob's trap door, the TIC and KET targets, the Spider, Drone and Wrench targets, the Lite Big Bang and Big Bang targets, the drone rubber switches, the jet bumpers and the skill shot inserts. The Big Bang target and the trap door are the weakest.

Sources: Rulesheet, JJP's game manual (section A.2, rules and shot maps), JJP's rules flow chart, JJP's model comparison sheet, and the Limited Edition playfield photo from Jersey Jack Pinball.
